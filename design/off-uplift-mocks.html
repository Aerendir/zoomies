Off status icon uplift: motion mocks

Your system asks for reduced motion, so the mocks are paused. Use the Motion button to play them anyway.

Current column is the shipped OffStatusIcon ported verbatim: the state's Lucide glyph in a bordered tile, moving only when work is in progress, keyed by glyph. Proposed keeps the philosophy and four of the seven motions unchanged (spin, turn, lift and the calm default). The changes: breathe no longer dims to 55% opacity, which reads as disabled, and gently scales instead; sag finally does what its comment promises, tugged down and dragging itself back up, so it stops looking like lift's twin; the trace pulse is normalised with pathLength so it no longer depends on magic numbers tuned to one glyph's length; and buzz is compressed from nineteen keyframes to eight with its flash capped at 1.12. One further recommendation outside this mock: an in-progress glyph missing from the motion map silently holds still today and looks finished; the map should fall back to a quiet default for busy-tone glyphs rather than to stillness.
